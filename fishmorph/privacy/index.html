Fishmorph

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Fishmorph (“the Game”) is developed by Reefpunch. This policy explains what data the Game handles. Package name: com.reefpunch.fishmorph.

Data we collect ourselves

None. The Game has no accounts, no login and no servers of our own. Your progress — levels, stars, pearls, upgrades, skins and settings — is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the Game.

Data collected by third parties

Google AdMob (advertising)

The Game shows interstitial ads and opt-in rewarded ads. AdMob may collect a device advertising identifier, approximate (non-precise) location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction data in order to serve and measure ads. See Google’s advertising policy.

Google Play Billing (in-app purchase)

The one-time “Fishmorph Plus” purchase is handled by Google Play. Payment details are processed by Google; we never see or store them. See Google’s privacy policy.

Children

The Game contains no gambling content and is suitable for all ages. Where required, ads are requested with child-directed and non-personalised settings enabled.

Your choices

  • Reset or limit ad personalisation: Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Remove all local data: uninstall the Game, or Settings → Apps → Fishmorph → Clear storage.
  • Buying “Fishmorph Plus” removes interstitial ads.

Contact

Questions about this policy: reefpunch.games@gmail.com

Changes

We will update this page and the date above if this policy changes.
